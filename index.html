Karambe Village
Water Run

Three levels. Three cans each. Fastest total time wins.

Move: hold ◀ / ▶. Keep moving into a ladder to climb. Follow the short path downhill to water.
CAN: tap to place ahead or pick up. Hold at water to fill; hold at the village to deliver.
Full can: take the long path left of water. To jump, put it down, jump, then retrieve it.
Rocks (L2+): face the rock and hold CAN to block, or place the can in its path.
Snakes (L3): jump onto them or place a full can ahead to crush them.
Fast retry: delivered cans stay banked. Only time is lost. Finish all three levels to unlock Level Select.
Turn the phone upright to play Karambe Village Water Run.
